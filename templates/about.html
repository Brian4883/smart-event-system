Our Story

Built by Event
Lovers, for Event Lovers

EventSphere started with a single frustrating experience — trying to manage a 300-person conference using a combination of spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and manual check-in lists. We knew there had to be a better way.

Founded in 2022, we set out to build the platform we wished existed — one that makes professional event management accessible to everyone, from solo creators hosting their first workshop to enterprise teams running global summits.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Community First

Events bring people together. Every feature we build starts with the question: does this help organisers create better experiences for their community?

Trust & Transparency

No hidden fees, no surprise charges. We publish our pricing clearly and treat your data with the respect it deserves under GDPR and local regulations.

Speed & Reliability

Events don't wait. Our infrastructure is built for 99.9% uptime so your ticketing page never goes down during peak sales moments.

Data-Driven Decisions

We give you the same quality analytics that enterprise tools charge thousands for — because smart data should be available to everyone.

Built for Africa

M-Pesa integration, mobile-first design, and local support — because the fastest-growing event market in the world deserves tools that fit it.

Delight in the Details

From the smoothness of QR check-in to the clarity of our invoices — we obsess over the small things because attendees notice them too.
